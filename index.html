A Decentralized Data Wallet Unlocking Individual Data into the Next Digital Asset

Unlocking Decentralized Personal Insights (DPIs) as the next digital asset.

DataTrek is a Decentralized Data Wallet (DDW) empowering individuals to own, govern, and monetize behavioral data without sacrificing privacy.

The Problem: A Broken Data Economy

  • Individuals: Generate valuable behavioral data but lack ownership, control, and receives zero financial return.
  • Flawed Monetization: Current markets trade raw personal data, exposing privacy, rather than monetizing secure, actionable insights.
  • AI Black-Box: Centralized analytics rely on opaque servers, forcing users to blindly trust unverified algorithmic execution.
  • Enterprises: Face the problem of weak provenance, rely on opaque data brokers, resulting in polluted datasets and unreliable risk assessments.

Our Solution: The "Data-to-Asset" Pipeline

  • Eliminating the Middleman: A trustless Web3 ecosystem that connects individuals directly to enterprises.
  • AI Extraction: Users upload raw travel receipts; AI extracts the data locally.
  • Privacy-by-Design: Smart contracts convert raw data into Decentralized Personal Insights (DPIs). Enterprises purchase access to mathematical insights (like Credit Scores), never the underlying personal data.

Methodology

1. Multivocal Literature Review (MLR)

  • Synthesize academic and industry research.
  • Establish system architecture and tokenomics blueprint.

2. Evolutionary Prototyping

  • Build a dual-interface Web3 dApp (Contributor & Consumer portals).
  • Deploy seamlessly on the Ethereum Sepolia Testnet.

3. On-Chain Machine Learning

  • Feature Engineering: Mapped raw OCR metadata into optimized integer variables for smart contract ingestion.
  • Gas Optimization: Restricted tree depth (max depth 3) to strictly comply with Ethereum block gas and EIP-170 size limits.
  • Model Transpilation: Converted the trained machine learning models directly into native Solidity smart contracts.
  • Deterministic Output: Enabled the zero-exposure, on-chain generation of the Alternative Credit and Health Oracles.
  • Off-Chain Backtesting: Trained and validated Decision Trees off-chain using public datasets to ensure high predictive accuracy.

4. System Evaluations

  • Alpha Testing: Conducted internal technical stress-testing.
  • Beta Testing: Ran User-Centered Design trials using real-world data.

ML Algorithm for DPI Generation

Choosing a Decision Tree for our on-chain engine was a strategic pivot driven by the unique constraints of the blockchain environment. While ensemble models like Random Forests are powerful off-chain, they are computationally "heavy" and often exceed Ethereum's gas and contract size limits. By transitioning to a lightweight, deterministic Decision Tree, we achieved a high-performance "White Box" architecture.

This approach ensures that complex risk assessments—like our Credit and Health Oracles—can execute natively on-chain with minimal gas fees and zero aggregation latency. The result is a fully transparent, verifiable model where the logic is hardcoded into the smart contract, providing enterprise-grade reliability without compromising decentralized principles.

Real-Time Inference & Score Synthesis

The ML engine processes engineered features through transpiled smart contracts to generate two distinct, verifiable Decentralised Personal Insights (DPIs).

1. Alternative Credit Score

Model Architecture:

3-Level Decision Tree (Max 8 terminal leaves)

Synthesis Formula:

Score = 300 + [ (Probability × 550) / 100 ]

Risk Stratification:

  • Excellent (740-850): Exceptional financial capacity.
  • Good (670-739): Moderate reliability and stable patterns.
  • Fair (580-669): Average reliability; minor data gaps.
  • Poor (300-579): High volatility or low transparency.

2. Health & Wellbeing Index

Model Architecture:

2-Level Piecewise Function (Max 4 terminal leaves)

Synthesis Logic:

Discrete Index (45, 65, 85, 98) based on creditAmount and age.

Insight Categorization:

  • Highly Optimal (98): Superior preparedness and lifestyle stability.
  • Optimal (85): High preparedness and active compliance.
  • Stable (65): Average stability; occasional risk gaps.
  • Vulnerable (45): High Risk; low preparedness and chronic volatility.
